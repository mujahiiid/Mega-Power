# Delta Aman Engineering & Contracting

دلتا أمان للهندسة والمقاولات is a fictional Egyptian B2B brand created for this website demonstration. The name follows common local patterns that combine a geographic reference with a clear Arabic value and a direct engineering descriptor. Exact English and Arabic name searches did not surface a well-known Egyptian company using this identity at the time of research.

## Market references

The service vocabulary and site structure were informed by public Egyptian market examples:

- GEMC: HVAC, fire protection, power and control systems.
- S&PA: design, supply, installation and maintenance of fire protection, fire alarm and HVAC systems.
- Kinetic Egypt: turnkey HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire protection work.
- AMEC: MEP, facility management, fire systems and engineering support in 6th of October City.
- ECS: electrical, mechanical, fire alarm, fire fighting, light current and infrastructure scopes.

No company identity, logo, client list, certification or project claim was copied. All project-style content is explicitly presented as representative scope.
